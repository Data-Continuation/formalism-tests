# Stage 19 — Candidate Review, Acceptance, Rejection and Supersession Loop

Stage 19 defines the lifecycle for candidate artifacts generated by the governed work‑entity.  After drafting candidates in Stage 18, `StegVerse‑001` must submit them into a **governed review loop**.  Each candidate is either accepted (`ALLOW_REVIEW_ENTRY`), rejected (`LEDGER_REJECTION`), superseded (`LEDGER_SUPERSESSION`), or fails closed (`FAIL_CLOSED`).

The fixture covers multiple review outcomes:

- **Review entry:** A candidate with complete receipts, closed dependencies, and authorized scope may enter the review queue (`ALLOW_REVIEW_ENTRY`).
- **Rejection:** Missing receipts, dependency violations, or unauthorized scopes cause the candidate to be rejected and logged on the ledger (`LEDGER_REJECTION`).
- **Supersession:** If a newer candidate replaces an older one, the superseded candidate is recorded (`LEDGER_SUPERSESSION`).  Supersession can also occur when policy requirements change or multiple drafts are merged.
- **Failures:** Candidates lacking quorum approval, exhibiting hidden authority, or missing lineage continuity are failed closed.

This loop ensures that candidate artifacts cannot progress without explicit acceptance, preserves a record of rejections and supersessions, and enforces negative controls on unauthorized or ill‑formed proposals.
